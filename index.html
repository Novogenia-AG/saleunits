Elements – Costs and Prices

0

Every point listed on the customer card under “What is included” is an element. Enter the cost and net price per element; the margin is calculated next to it. Product Financials shows the sum of its elements for each unit; without an own net price this sum applies. Saved in this browser (and in the JSON export).

Price Lists

0

A price list bundles sale units with two prices: the price to the tenant (net, what we invoice the tenant) and the price to the end customer (net, based on the net price from Products and Prices). The difference is the tenant's margin. Empty fields are calculated from the list's condition. Price lists are assigned to tenants in the “Tenants and Price Lists” tab; there you can override prices per tenant and add further products.

Invoices

0

All collective invoices and drafts created here. Change the status per invoice; drafts and created (not yet sent) invoices can be opened and changed. Sent invoices are fixed – changes only via cancellation and a new invoice. Cancelled invoices release their orders and the credit used. Saved in this browser (and in the JSON export).

Tenants and Price Lists

0

One row per tenant: who receives the invoice? With “Tenant” the billing address from Business Central is shown next to it. Credit = balance the tenant has prepaid; enter it with amount and date via “+ Credit”. It is used up by collective invoices on which the credit is applied. Click the column headers to sort. The status rule defines which order statuses trigger “to be invoiced”; clicking the numbers shows the statuses per tenant, the invoice icon opens the collective invoice. To be invoiced = invoiced orders / open orders without an invoice according to BC. Source: BC customers of the channel and partner ranges (22x, 259, 260, 270, 29x) and NovoServices tenants from the BC orders, . NovoCore is still missing. Credit and selections are saved in this browser (and in the JSON export).
